Got Questions?

FAQ

Where does the money go?

+

100% of proceeds from ticket sales and donations go directly to Transcend Sarcoma, a charity dedicated to raising awareness and funding research into sarcoma.

Is the rooftop party included with my ticket?

+

Yes. The Yellow Park rooftop finish party is included with every ticket — Full Challenge, Marathon + Murph, Half Marathon, and Murph Only. You can also buy a standalone rooftop party ticket.

Do I need to run the full 84km?

+

No. Choose your level — a half marathon (21km), the full marathon + Murph (42km), the Murph workout only, or even just the rooftop party.

What is the Murph workout?

+

The Murph is a legendary CrossFit Hero workout: 1 mile run, 100 pull-ups, 200 press-ups, 300 air squats, then another 1 mile run. It's performed at Newwave CrossFit Byfleet. All fitness levels welcome — scale as needed.

Where does the event start and finish?

+

Both start and finish are at Yellow Park in Wapping, London. The turnaround point is Newwave CrossFit on Redhill Road, Byfleet, Surrey.

Will there be hydration support on the route?

+

Yes. Checkpoints with hydration and nutrition will be positioned along the route, including at Sheen Gate (Richmond Park) and Newwave CrossFit Byfleet.

Can I combine segments or add merchandise?

+

Yes. During checkout you can select multiple segments and add merchandise to your booking. You can also purchase merch separately.

What's included in the rooftop party?

+

The rooftop finish party at Yellow Park includes access from 17:00, pizza, a welcome drink, and sunset views over the London skyline. Additional drinks available at the bar.
